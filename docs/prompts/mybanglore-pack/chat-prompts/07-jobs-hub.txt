Read AGENTS.md, docs/FILE_MAP.md, and database/schema.sql before writing. Use the existing `employers`, `job_postings`, and `job_seeker_posts` tables. A public job is open only when `status = 'open'` and `valid_through` is null or still in the future. Hide expired rows from the hub, sitemap, search, area pages, and JSON-LD. ## Routes

- `/bengaluru-jobs` — open jobs hub
- `/bengaluru-jobs/looking-for-work` — people available for hire
- `/bengaluru-jobs/{slug}` — one employer listing
- `/bengaluru-jobs/looking-for-work/{slug}` — one seeker profile
- `/bengaluru-jobs/feed.xml` — RSS of open jobs only
- `/bengaluru-jobs/{slug}/markdown` and `/bengaluru-jobs/looking-for-work/{slug}/markdown` — plain markdown of that open row, for readers and agents
- `/advertise/jobs` — employer pitch. No login. Sends people to `/contact#jobs` and WhatsApp only after the existing join or desk rules
- `/desk` jobs screens — list, create, edit, close expired, and call-unlock requests

Register the looking-for-work paths before the `{slug}` path so “looking-for-work” is never treated as a job slug. Closed, draft, and expired rows 404. Unknown slugs 404.

## Desk

On `/desk`, add a jobs list: status, title, employer, area, valid through, open or closed. Create and edit the real columns. Publishing sets `status = open` and `published_at` if empty. “Close expired” sets `closed` on rows that are still `open` but past `valid_through`. A dry view lists the slugs before the write.

A separate view lists call-unlock rows: when, which listing, the visitor mobile. It is not a public page. Do not put publish buttons on an analytics view if you split views and editing the way Chennai does.

If `job_phone_reveals` does not exist, add only that table: id, job_posting_id nullable, job_seeker_post_id nullable, visitor_phone, created_at. One of the two listing ids is required.

## Editorial rules (do these on every listing)

- Quote a male-only or female-only line as written. Do not add the other group to be polite.
- “Best in industry”, “as per market”, or “23K gross” with no pay period is body text. It is not a JobPosting salary.
- A stated monthly figure under ₹1.2 lakh may be stored as monthly min and max when the note states the period or the desk treats a shop band that way. Say the basis in the body.
- Do not turn an email domain into a company name. The employer is the name on the poster or note.
- A person may list without a company or GST number.
- Keep every trade and every named neighbourhood that the note put on one poster on one listing.
- Point the apply button at the company’s public careers page when one exists. Otherwise use the channel the note printed, in this order when several exist: the channel the note tells the candidate to use. Do not add WhatsApp when the note only says call.
- Re-saving a listing whose `valid_through` is already past must save it as `closed`.
- Area hubs may list open jobs whose `area_id` or location text matches that hub. Expired jobs disappear there too.

## SEO

Title pattern `{Segment} · mybanglore.in`. Hub title segment: “Jobs in Bengaluru”. Description includes the live open count when it is above zero, and says to apply on the employer’s own page. Canonical is the apex URL. Hub alternates include the RSS feed. Detail title uses the listing headline, not a shortened role. Open Graph image is the site default. No JSON-LD on an empty hub. Sitemap lists only open, unexpired job and seeker URLs.

## Empty and error states

Database failure renders the empty hub, not a stack trace. Zero jobs hides the homepage jobs block, as the homepage contract already says. The jobs hub itself still renders, with the empty copy, so employers can find Post a job.

Expired jobs 404 and are absent from the feed, sitemap, and schema. `/advertise/jobs` does not publish by itself. The desk can close expired rows. No Chennai name, area, phone, or partner creative remains on these pages.
