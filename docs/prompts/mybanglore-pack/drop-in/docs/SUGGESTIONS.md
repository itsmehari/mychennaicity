# Further suggestions — not in the build yet

English stays the only interface. These are the next city-layer ideas. Do not build them until the operator accepts one.

## Worth doing soon

1. **New here.** An English page, `/guides/moved-to-bengaluru`, with doors to the rent, commute, water, and khata guides, plus a line on PG versus a flat. No invented document checklist.
2. **Two doors on the jobs hub.** “Hiring” and “Looking for work”. The seeker table already exists. An empty seeker list should say so. Office roles and site, shop, and delivery roles stay on one hiring list.
3. **PG first, when classifieds open.** The first categories to prepare are PG, room, and flatmate. A generic ad board can wait.
4. **Area pages stay qualitative.** One sentence on the kind of place (old grid, pete, office belt, industrial). No typical rent, no safety score, no “IT crowd” label.

## Worth doing later

5. **Lakes as a news topic, then a guide.** Bellandur, Varthur, Ulsoor, and Sankey are names residents already use. Link an official pollution or corporation page when you have the right URL. Do not publish live water-quality numbers.
6. **Rides.** Do not build a fare calculator. A short note can say: meter, app, and confirm the fare before you sit. Namma Yatri can be named as a local app without becoming a partner.
7. **Directory types to add when the directory opens.** `pg` and `darshini` (tiffin room). Skip a food game or a coffee map.
8. **Events only with a real date.** Kadalekai Parishe, Karaga, Rajyotsava, Lalbagh shows, and a Chinnaswamy match day as a traffic note. The seasonal band stays “how this desk works” until one of those is sourced.
9. **Rain.** Bengaluru today may link to the IMD page. It should not scrape a forecast.
10. **Readers who do not read Kannada.** Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu speakers are a large part of the city. They use the English site. Do not open three more language sites in v1.
11. **Colleges.** A directory entry is enough. Do not host syllabi or a university timetable.
12. **Women-only lines.** If a PG or job note says women only, quote it as printed. Do not add a safety ranking.

## Leave alone

The visual system, the database shape, and the English interface stay as they are. Ward boundaries stay unpublished until an official file is in the repo.
